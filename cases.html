Retention · Revenue

The journey that became CRM's biggest revenue line

B2C Edtech scale-up

R$3.8M
revenue · 20% of total CRM revenue

Context

Subscription renewal relied on generic, poorly-timed communication, leaving revenue on the table at a critical point in the customer lifecycle.

Approach

I designed automated Renewal and Reactivation journeys combining email, push, pop-up, and WhatsApp, with behavior-based personalization and continuous optimization through A/B testing.

Result

The journeys generated R$3.8M in revenue in 2023 — 20% of the year's total CRM revenue. WhatsApp alone accounted for R$1.5M (39% of flow-driven sales). The campaign's emails hit the year's highest open and click-through rates, outperforming even Black Friday.

Automation · Scale

Building the QC Journey: from 0 to 50+ flows

B2C Edtech scale-up

50+
flows · double-digit YoY growth for 3 years

Context

The CRM operation needed to move from one-off campaigns to an automation architecture covering the entire customer journey — without losing visibility into per-flow performance.

Approach

I structured the "QC Journey": 50+ automated, personalized flows organized around AARRR metrics (acquisition, activation, retention, revenue, referral), with executive dashboards in Looker Studio tracking each stage.

Result

The initiative sustained double-digit year-over-year growth for 3 consecutive years in the CRM operation.
