About

A little about me

The person behind the campaigns, automations, and spreadsheets full of cohort data.

Hey, I'm Aron Henriquez 👋

I'm a Senior CRM & Lifecycle Marketing Analyst based in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. For the past 7+ years, I've been working at tech companies across Latin America — building the kind of marketing infrastructure that helps businesses communicate better with their users.

My work sits at the intersection of strategy, data, and execution. I get excited about customer journeys, behavioral segmentation, and the kind of A/B test that actually teaches you something useful. I've had the chance to build CRM operations from the ground up, and to optimize ones that were already running — both require very different skills, and I enjoy both.

Over the past 4+ years, I've specialized in CRM and Lifecycle Marketing, leading acquisition, activation, and retention campaigns across Brazil, Mexico, Colombia, and Argentina. I've worked with tools like Iterable, Braze, HubSpot, Insider, Segment, Amplitude, Blip, Sinch, and more. But honestly, the tools matter less than the thinking behind how you use them.

Before going deep into CRM, I spent time in Sales — which gave me a real appreciation for the full funnel. Understanding how leads become customers, and what makes some of them stick around while others churn, became the thread that connects everything I've done since.

Outside of work, I'm someone who is curious about a lot of things. I started this site to have a space where I can share ideas, document what I learn, and occasionally write about topics that have nothing to do with marketing at all — because I believe good thinking in one area tends to make you better in another.

Education

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Business Administration from Centro Universitário La Salle (2019–2022), where I built the analytical and strategic foundation I apply every day. I've continued my education through platforms like DataCamp, studying SQL, Power BI, data analysis, and AI fundamentals.

Why this site?

I built this site to be my digital home — a place that's more honest and complete than a LinkedIn profile. You'll find my full work history, the tools and skills I've developed, and my writing. If you're a recruiter, a future collaborator, or just someone curious about what I do, I hope this gives you a real sense of who I am and how I think.
